VSU CLEANROOM & ESD PROTOCOL

    Desktop: click each station under the orange marker, in order. WASD to walk, arrow keys to turn.
    VR: squeeze the grip at a dispenser to take the item, then put it on: booties to your feet, bouffant on your head, coverall to your chest, mask to your face, gloves and wrist strap to your other hand. Left stick moves, right stick turns.
